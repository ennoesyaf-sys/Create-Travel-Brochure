Group roles

Groups of three: combine Canva Designer and Editor & Presenter. Everyone reviews the final work.

Researcher

Finds key facts and records the information sources.

English Writer

Drafts the text and checks the Passive Voice sentences.

Canva Designer

Chooses a template and arranges text and images.

Editor & Presenter

Checks facts, spelling and readability, then introduces the brochure.
